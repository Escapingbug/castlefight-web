retimed their vertex animation and adapted the materials for Godot, including
team colors and foliage transparency. The adapted assets are offered under

Glest Mapcraft converted these XML emitters to Godot CPU particle scenes.
The shared particle mask, color and size endpoints, and flight path types
come from the original recipes. Emission counts, lifetimes, spread, gravity,
and some trajectory amplitudes were adapted to the arena camera and pacing.
The adapted scenes are offered under CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported.
