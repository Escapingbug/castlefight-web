Effekseer combat animations for Ashfall Survivors
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The PNG sheets are offline renders of original Effekseer samples. The effects
were exported with Effekseer 1.80.8, captured at 30 FPS, and cropped and packed
into 128 × 128 cells, eight cells per row. Projectile and loot loops use
repeated or reversed source frames. No engine plug-in is required to play
the resulting PNG sheets.
